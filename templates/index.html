Summary of our Privacy Policy

EssayMind is a coursework project by Carl Adrian Baldonado (2CPE-A). By using this app, you acknowledge:

  • No personal data collected — no name, email, or account required. An anonymous browser cookie identifies your session.
  • Essay text is processed externally — submitted essays are sent to Groq API (AI evaluation) and ZeroGPT (AI detection) over HTTPS. Do not submit sensitive or confidential documents.
  • Local history only — evaluation history is stored on an ephemeral server and may be lost on restart. Local preferences are saved in your browser.
  • You can delete your sessions — use the trash icon in the sidebar at any time.
  • No passwords or API keys are ever collected from you.

For the full policy, visit essaymind/privacy. Contact: baldonadocarl12345@gmail.com
